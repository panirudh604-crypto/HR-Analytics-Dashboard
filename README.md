# HR-Analytics-Dashboard
This project explores employee data to understand workforce composition, hiring trends, salary distribution and employee performance.
Using Microsoft Excel, I analyzed a synthetic HR dataset containing 10,000 employee records and developed a single-page interactive dashboard to present key workforce metrics and uncover meaningful insights.

🎯 Project Objectives:

• Examine employee distribution across departments and demographics.
• Identify hiring trends over time.
• Analyze workforce distribution by years of experience.
• Compare average gross salaries across departments.
• Understand employee performance rating patterns.
• Monitor workforce metrics through interactive filters.

📊 Dashboard Analysis:

The dashboard focuses on six key areas:

1. Employee Distribution by Department: Compares employee counts across departments.
2. Gender Distribution: Examines the gender composition of the workforce.
3. Employee Hiring Trend: Tracks hiring patterns over the years.
4. Experience Distribution: Explores employee distribution across experience levels.
5. Average Gross Salary by Department: Compares average gross salaries across departments.
6. Performance Rating Distribution: Examines the distribution of employee performance ratings.

📈 Key Metrics:

• Total Employees: 10,000
• Active Employees: 8,488 (84.88%)
• Gross Payroll: 755.5M
• Average Work Experience: 6 Years
• Average Performance Rating: 3.78

🔍 Key Insights:

• Product has the highest employee count with 1,166 employees.
• The workforce has a nearly equal gender split with 4,983 male and 5,017 female employees.
• 2017 recorded the highest number of hires with 903 employees.
• Employees with 8 years of experience form the largest group with 901 employees.
• Customer Service has the highest average gross salary at 76,908.34.
• A rating of 3.9 is the most common recorded for 641 employees.

It also helped me understand the additional effort involved in building interactive dashboards in Excel, particularly in managing slicer connections, arranging visuals and maintaining a consistent layout compared to Power BI.
